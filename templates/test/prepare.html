Before You Begin

Proper preparation is essential for accurate muscle testing, as taught by Dr. David R. Hawkins. Please review each item:

🫀

Thymic Thump

Thump your upper breastbone firmly three times with your fingertips, while smiling and thinking of something or someone you love. This clears energetic imbalances in the thymus meridian and resets your system for accurate testing.

🔇

Quiet Environment

Turn off background music, TV, podcasts, and notifications. Find a quiet space. External noise and distraction interfere with the subtle muscle response. Even a phone screen nearby can throw off results.

🧘

Emotional Neutrality

Take a few deep breaths. Release strong emotional charges. The test responds to truth, not preference — let go of attachment to outcomes.

🙏

Prayer as Mudra

Bring your palms together at heart center in Anjali Mudra (prayer position). Close your eyes, take three slow deep breaths, and state silently with intention: "I am open to truth, whatever it may be." This hand position aligns body and mind for honest inquiry.
