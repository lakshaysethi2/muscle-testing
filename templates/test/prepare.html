Before You Begin

Proper preparation is essential for accurate muscle testing, as taught by Dr. David R. Hawkins. Please review each item:

💧

Hydration

Drink a glass of water. Hawkins emphasized hydration for proper electrical conductivity through the body's meridians.

🧘

Emotional Neutrality

Take a few deep breaths. Release strong emotional charges. The test responds to truth, not preference — let go of attachment to outcomes.

🫁

Centering Exercise

Close your eyes. Take 3 slow, deep breaths. Feel your feet on the ground. State silently: "I am open to truth, whatever it may be."

👥

Testing Partner

If using the two-person O-ring method, you will need a partner. Self-testing methods are available in the next step if solo.
